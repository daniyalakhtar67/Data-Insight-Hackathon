ColdChain Watch Pakistan — Data Source Verification

Project objective: Build a self-refreshing data pipeline to help Pakistani cold-store managers monitor external heat exposure and analyse short-term price trends for tomatoes, onions, and potatoes.

Source 1: Open-Meteo Weather API

- Publisher/provider: Open-Meteo
- Data: Hourly temperature, relative humidity, and other weather variables for locations such as Okara and Multan.
- Official API: https://open-meteo.com/
- Terms: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
- Licence: Weather data under CC BY 4.0, with attribution.
- Access conditions: The free API is restricted to non-commercial use, with a limit of 10,000 API calls per day. Commercial applications require an appropriate paid plan.
- Pipeline role: Historical weather backfill, recent weather ingestion, and external heat-exposure analysis.
- Verification status: Official source and licence conditions verified.

Source 2: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics — Sensitive Price Indicator (SPI)

- Publisher: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Government of Pakistan.
- Data: Weekly price statistics for essential commodities, including tomatoes, onions, and potatoes.
- Official website: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/blog/
- Example report: https://www.pbs.gov.pk/sites/default/files/price_statistics/weekly_spi/SPI%20Executive%20Summary%20%26%20SPI%20Report_04.09.2025.pdf
- Frequency: Weekly publications.
- Pipeline role: Historical price analysis and short-term price forecasting.
- Licence and reuse conditions: To be checked against applicable PBS terms before redistribution.
- Verification status: Official publication series verified; crop-level price granularity, historical consistency, and reuse conditions require further validation.

Optional Source 3: WFP Food Prices — HDX

- Dataset page: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/wfp-food-prices-for-pakistan
- Proposed role: Supplementary historical price data, if the live dataset is suitable.
- Verification status: Current coverage, crop availability, licence, update frequency, and provenance are not yet confirmed. Excluded from the core source count until verified.

Methodological notes

1. Weather and price data will be ingested into Bronze, cleaned and validated in Silver, and transformed into model-ready Gold tables.
2. Weather-based heat exposure and internal cold-store temperature will be treated as different concepts.
3. Initial baselines will be same-hour-last-week for weather-based exposure and last-observed-price for price forecasting.
4. Price forecasts will be evaluated against the weekly reporting cadence, rather than pretending that weekly prices are observed hourly.
5. Crop-specific storage thresholds will be documented using authoritative post-harvest guidance.
6. Data quality, missing values, duplicates, timestamps, units, provenance, and data freshness will be tracked throughout the pipeline.

Primary sources selected: Open-Meteo and PBS. Both are credible sources for their respective data types, subject to the stated access and validation requirements.
